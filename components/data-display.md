# Ledgers, sheets, slips, and charts

## Ledgers

Ledgers use semantic rows, strong headers, and source-visible state labels.

Ruled rows communicate relationships more clearly than repeated generic cards.

Dense tables must have a compact reading order on small screens.

## Paper sheets and slips

Sheets use paper, soft paper, or mist surfaces with one-pixel rules.

Slips may expose a compact evidence summary and one clear action.

Do not use decorative cards when a ruled row communicates the same relationship.

## Charts

Charts use ink lines, pale grids, paper backgrounds, and seal points for current focus.

Every chart needs a written title, a readable summary, and a non-visual equivalent when the data is important.

Charts must distinguish missing data from zero.
